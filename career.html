A LEGENDARY PATH

Across four countries and five world-class clubs, Ronaldo has redefined what it means to be a professional athlete. His tenure at Real Madrid saw him average more than a goal per game, a feat rarely seen in modern football.

Key Milestones:

  • Manchester United: Won his first Ballon d'Or and Champions League.
  • Real Madrid: Became the club's all-time top scorer with 450 goals.
  • Juventus: Conquered Italy, winning multiple Serie A titles.
  • Al-Nassr: Leading the football revolution in the Middle East.
